# ShashwatGold1.github.io

Serves one file: [`app-ads.txt`](app-ads.txt), for the **Dhun** Android app.

AdMob checks the root of the developer website listed on Google Play
(`https://shashwatgold1.github.io/app-ads.txt`) to confirm which ad accounts may sell ads
in the app. Without it, ad serving is limited.

The policy pages live in a separate repository, `dhun-policy`, at
`https://shashwatgold1.github.io/dhun-policy/`. Do not move them here — those links are
already public.
